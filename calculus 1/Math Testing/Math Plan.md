**Card 2 — Limits and Limit Laws (Ch. 2.1–2.2)**

- **Book/sections:** OpenStax, Ch. 2.1–2.2
    
- **Objective:** Evaluate limits numerically and algebraically; recognize when limits do not exist.
    
- **Why this chapter:** Limits are the gateway to derivatives and common trap area on finals.
    
- **Prereqs to recall:** Function notation; evaluating functions at points and near points.
    
- **Key terms/theorems:** Limit from the left/right, $\lim_{x\to a} f(x)$, limit laws.
    
- **Worked examples to replicate:**
    
    - Limits from tables and graphs
        
    - Algebraic simplification (factoring, cancellation)
        
- **Exercises set (ranges):** 8–12 problems: ~4 graphical, ~4 algebraic, 2 conceptual “does not exist” cases.
    
- **Deliverable:** A table with 6 limits: for each, method used (graph/table/algebra) and 1-sentence explanation.
    
- **Mastery check (timed):** 15-minute set of 5 mixed limits; ≥4 correct.
    
- **Estimated time:** 1 × 75-min session + 15-min quiz (≈1 h 30 min).
    

---

**Card 3 — Continuity and Special Limits (Ch. 2.3–2.4)**

- **Book/sections:** OpenStax, Ch. 2.3–2.4 (precise limit, continuity)
    
- **Objective:** Recognize continuity; connect removable/jump/infinite discontinuities to graphs and formulas.
    
- **Why this chapter:** Many derivative questions hide continuity tests and removable discontinuities.
    
- **Prereqs to recall:** Informal notion of limit; piecewise functions.
    
- **Key terms/theorems:** Continuity at a point, types of discontinuity, $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ idea (intuitive only).
    
- **Worked examples to replicate:**
    
    - Checking continuity at a point
        
    - Fixing a removable discontinuity by defining $f(a)$
        
- **Exercises set:** 8–10 problems, at least 3 piecewise continuity checks.
    
- **Deliverable:** One page with 3 worked continuity problems and labeled type of discontinuity each time.
    
- **Mastery check (timed):** 10-minute, 3-question continuity quiz; ≥2 correct.
    
- **Estimated time:** 1 × 75-min session + 10-min quiz (≈1 h 25 min).

### Definitions

- week window: the total span of calendar weeks you’re planning for (here: all weeks up to the final).
    
- Deep Study block: a pre-reserved, interruption-free chunk of time in your weekly schedule.
    
- chapter plan card: a structured checklist for mastering one chunk of textbook material.
    
- mastery check: a short, timed test for one topic with a clear pass threshold (usually ≥80%).
    
- mock final: a full-length practice exam under timed conditions, covering the entire course.
    
- slip policy: rules for how to reschedule unfinished work without panicking or cramming.
